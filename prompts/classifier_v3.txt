You are classifying Instagram comments from a real Instagram comment dataset. Many comments are written in Tunisian Arabic (Darija), either in Arabic script or in Latin letters with numbers (for example 3 = ع, 7 = ح, 9 = ق), mixed with French and English.

Classify each comment into exactly one of these categories:

Positive
The comment clearly expresses appreciation, praise, affection, support, encouragement, admiration, agreement, or another genuinely positive reaction.

Humour
The main purpose of the comment is playful or funny. This includes jokes, playful teasing, irony, absurd or exaggerated statements, mock-serious statements, and clearly playful reactions.

Hate Speech
The comment is primarily insulting, degrading, offensive, hostile, or hateful toward a person or group. This includes:
- profanity and vulgar insults (including sexual insults and insults to someone's mother or family)
- religious insults (for example calling someone ungodly)
- calling people stupid, liars, or fake
- mocking, resentful or hostile attacks on a group, for example rich students, people showing off wealth or cars, or people called materialistic

There is no separate "criticism" label. Hostile, negative or mocking criticism of a person or group counts as Hate Speech, even if it is not a classic insult.

Tunisian Darija vocabulary (Latin letters or Arabic script):

- nik omk = f*** your mother; nik rohy = f*** me/myself (strong profanity)
- kroz = vulgar word, often used to mean "rich people" in a hostile way
- kofar / kofr = ungodly, disbeliever (insult)

- wtf = what the f***

Important classification rules:

1. Positive is NOT the default category.
Do not choose Positive simply because the comment is not obviously hateful. The comment must contain a genuinely positive reaction, such as praise, appreciation, affection, support, encouragement, or admiration.

2. Do not assume a comment is harmless because you do not understand it.
If it contains Darija, slang or vulgar words, use the vocabulary above. If a word is vulgar or insulting, the comment is probably Hate Speech even if it is short and has a laughing emoji.

3. Humour is about the main purpose of the comment.
A comment can be short and still be humorous. Look for joking, playful wording, exaggeration, irony, teasing, absurdity, or a deliberately funny statement. Short sad or neutral comments with no joke and no praise are not automatically Humour or Positive.

4. Do not assume that emojis automatically mean Humour.
Laughing or reaction emojis can accompany Positive comments or Hate Speech. Interpret emojis together with the words.

5. Distinguish playful teasing from hostility.
If the comment is mainly playful or joking, use Humour.
If the main purpose is insulting, degrading, mocking or attacking someone, use Hate Speech.

6. Do not invent context.
Use only information contained in the comment. If a joke would only be understandable from the video and the comment gives no indication of humour, do not invent a humorous interpretation.

7. When several categories seem possible, choose the category corresponding to the main communicative purpose of the comment.

8. Mentions such as @username do not determine the category by themselves. Classify the actual message surrounding the mention.
