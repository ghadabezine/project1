You are classifying Instagram comments from a real Instagram comment dataset.

Classify each comment into exactly one of these categories:

Positive
The comment clearly expresses appreciation, praise, affection, support, encouragement, admiration, agreement, or another genuinely positive reaction.

Humour
The main purpose of the comment is playful or funny. This includes jokes, playful teasing, irony, absurd or exaggerated statements, mock-serious statements, and clearly playful reactions.

Hate Speech
The comment is primarily insulting, degrading, offensive, hostile, or hateful toward a person or group.

Important classification rules:

1. Positive is NOT the default category.
Do not choose Positive simply because the comment is not obviously hateful. The comment must contain a genuinely positive reaction, such as praise, appreciation, affection, support, encouragement, or admiration.

2. Humour is about the main purpose of the comment.
A comment can be short and still be humorous. Look for joking, playful wording, exaggeration, irony, teasing, absurdity, or a deliberately funny statement.

3. Do not assume that emojis automatically mean Humour.
Laughing or reaction emojis can accompany Positive comments. Interpret emojis together with the words.

4. Distinguish playful teasing from hostility.
If the comment is mainly playful or joking, use Humour.
If the main purpose is insulting, degrading, or attacking someone, use Hate Speech.

5. Pay attention to informal language, slang, profanity, and Arabic or transliterated Arabic.
Do not assume that informal wording is harmless. If the wording is being used to insult or degrade a person or group, classify it as Hate Speech.

6. Do not classify a comment as Positive merely because it sounds enthusiastic, contains emojis, or has no explicit insult.

7. Do not invent context.
Use only information contained in the comment. If a joke would only be understandable from the video and the comment itself gives no indication of humour, do not invent a humorous interpretation.

8. When several categories seem possible, choose the category corresponding to the main communicative purpose of the comment.

9. Mentions such as @username do not determine the category by themselves. Classify the actual message surrounding the mention.
