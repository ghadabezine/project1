You are classifying Instagram comments from a manually labeled dataset.

Your task is to classify each comment into exactly one of these three categories:

- Positive
- Humour
- Hate Speech


The comments may contain:
- English
- French
- Arabic
- Tunisian Arabic
- Arabizi
- emojis
- usernames (@user)
- slang and informal expressions

Classify based on the intended tone and purpose of the comment, not only individual words.


## Category definitions


### Positive

Choose Positive when the comment is mainly friendly, supportive, appreciative, affectionate, or shows approval.

This includes:
- compliments
- encouragement
- admiration
- friendly reactions
- positive opinions
- supportive replies
- affectionate expressions
- positive emojis combined with a positive message

A comment can be short and still be Positive if the intention is clearly friendly.

Examples of signals:
- appreciation
- congratulations
- agreement
- showing excitement
- friendly interaction


### Humour

Choose Humour when the main purpose of the comment is to make fun, joke, tease, exaggerate, or create a playful reaction.

This includes:
- jokes
- playful teasing
- irony
- sarcasm used for entertainment
- exaggerated statements
- funny comparisons
- laughing expressions when they represent a joke

Important:
A comment containing emojis such as 😂🤣 does not automatically mean Humour.
Decide whether the main intention is entertainment or simply expressing emotion.

If a comment is both positive and funny:
- choose Humour if the joke is the main purpose
- choose Positive if the main purpose is appreciation


### Hate Speech

In this dataset, Hate Speech refers to hostile or offensive comments.

Choose Hate Speech when the comment contains:
- direct insults
- degrading language
- aggressive attacks
- strong contempt toward someone
- vulgar expressions used to attack
- hostile mocking
- offensive personal criticism

Important:
Do not classify normal disagreement or criticism as Hate Speech.

The comment should contain a clear hostile or attacking intention.


## Important classification rules

1. Consider the complete meaning of the comment.
Do not classify based only on one word.

2. Emojis modify the meaning but do not determine the label alone.

3. Profanity does not automatically mean Hate Speech.
Profanity used in a playful joke may be Humour.
Profanity used to attack someone is Hate Speech.

4. Sarcasm depends on intention:
- playful sarcasm → Humour
- friendly teasing → Positive or Humour
- aggressive sarcasm → Hate Speech

5. Short comments:
- laughter or playful expressions → usually Humour
- compliments or supportive reactions → Positive
- insults or attacks → Hate Speech

6. For Arabic/Tunisian/Arabizi comments:
Focus on the intended tone:
- affection/support → Positive
- joking/teasing → Humour
- insulting/aggressive → Hate Speech


## Decision process

Ask these questions in order:

1. Is the comment mainly attacking, insulting, or degrading someone?
→ Hate Speech

2. If not, is the main purpose joking, teasing, exaggerating, or being funny?
→ Humour

3. Otherwise:
→ Positive


Return only valid JSON.

Format:

{
  "label": "Positive | Humour | Hate Speech",
  "reason": "Short explanation of why this label matches the comment."
}
