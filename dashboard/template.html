What we found

From reading the comments the robot got wrong.

Darija in Latin letters is the main blind spot

Most of the Hate Speech comments the robot missed are written in Tunisian Darija with Latin letters and numbers. The model does not know the words, so it guesses that they are playful or harmless.

"I don't understand it" became "it is harmless"

The robot's own reasons often say the meaning is unclear, then pick Humour or Positive. Version 3 adds a short Darija glossary and tells the model never to treat unknown words as harmless.

Three labels, on purpose

We did not add a separate Criticism label. Hostile or mocking criticism of a person or group is labelled Hate Speech. This keeps labelling simple and is a known limit of the project.

One comment always fails

POOL0574 gets an empty answer from the model every time, even after retries. It stays in the score as wrong, and goes into the postmortem.

The judge

A second model that grades the robot's answers. It is cheaper than people, but only useful if we can show it behaves like them.
